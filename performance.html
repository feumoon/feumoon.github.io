ABOUT THE PERFORMANCE

What Have We Lost Sight Of? explores the relationship between the individual and the urban environment through moving images, sound, physical structures, and interaction.

Images of the city are projected onto a wall constructed from cardboard boxes. Touch and vibration destabilize both image and sound. The images shake, blur, and disappear, while urban noise gradually intensifies before giving way to silence.

Through the movement of images, sound, and the body, the work considers what we notice, what disappears from our sight, and what may still remain.
