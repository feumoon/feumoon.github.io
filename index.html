ABOUT

Fe:u Moon is a Korean visual artist working across painting, drawing, installation, performance, moving image, and interactive media.

Her practice begins with an attention to human presence — the traces we leave behind, what disappears, and what remains between the visible and the unseen.

Moving between physical materials, images, sound, and interactive systems, Moon explores how individuals experience relationships, environments, and the structures that surround them.

Rather than working within a fixed medium or a predetermined subject, she allows each work to develop from a question, sensation, or encounter.

Her practice moves between the personal and the social, between physical experience and mediated environments. Through this movement, she considers what we notice, what we lose sight of, and what may still remain with us.
