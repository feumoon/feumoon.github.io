ABOUT THE EXHIBITION

MoonLight in the Beginning brings together moving images, installation, light, and fragments of the human body. Eyes, faces, screens, and reflected light appear throughout the exhibition space, creating a shifting relationship between the viewer, the image, and the surrounding environment.
